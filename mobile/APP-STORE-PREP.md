# PaperMade — App Store / TestFlight Prep

## Product name

PaperMade

## Subtitle draft

Paper Trading on Live Crypto Charts

## Core description draft

PaperMade is a paper-trading companion for learning and testing crypto trading strategies on live chart pages without risking real funds.

The iPhone app acts as your PaperMade home for positions, journal history, P&L calendar, leaderboard, profiles, achievements and shareable simulated P&L cards.

The included Safari extension adds a touch-first PaperMade paper-trading panel to supported chart sites.

PaperMade does not require a seed phrase, private key, wallet custody or permission to sign blockchain transactions.

## Privacy / security positioning

- No wallet connection required
- No seed phrase or private key access
- No crypto custody
- No blockchain signing
- PaperMade account sync is used for simulated trading records
- App Group storage is used only to share PaperMade state between the PaperMade app and its Safari extension
