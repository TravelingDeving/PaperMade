# PaperMade Roadmap

PaperMade's roadmap is focused on learning, tracking, transparency, and community — not AI trade predictions or real-money auto trading.

## Phase 1 — Core paper overlay ✅
- Live chart tracking
- Paper buys and sells
- Virtual cash / position tracking
- Journal and After-I-Sold checkpoints
- Movable / resizable overlay
- Private-beta account sync
- Discord-gated access

## Phase 2 — Community foundation 🚧
- Global realized-P&L leaderboard
- P&L flex cards
- Public trader profiles
- Friends
- Trader comparisons
- Feedback / bug-report center
- Public transparency repository

## Phase 3 — Challenges + achievements
- Weekly competitions
- Win-streak badges
- Top-10 badges
- Consistency awards
- Challenge-specific leaderboards

## Phase 4 — Deeper performance analytics
- Equity curve
- Average winner vs average loser
- Drawdown
- Performance by hold time
- Performance by entry market cap
- Weekly recap cards

## Phase 5 — Public beta
- Polished installer
- Store-ready extension packaging
- Expanded privacy controls
- Broader community access
- Full public-source review of the private-beta authentication bridge
